THE CREATOR'S JOURNEY
Behind PROXIMUS The work, the progression and the vision

 

  
INDEPENDENT DEVELOPMENT · COMMUNITY TO CREATIVE TOOLS
Ideas turned into working systems.

   
Behind the PROXIMUS and NEXUS projects is an independent developer and creator known as ZevRo. The work began with a fascination for gaming, Discord communities, automation and building better ways for people to interact with software.

   
What started as individual utilities and server systems gradually expanded into a connected library of creative workspaces, desktop applications, browser tools and entertainment projects. Each release represents another step toward something more polished, more capable and easier to use.

  
WHERE THE JOURNEY GREW
From community systems to full creative applications

   
Early development centered on Discord management, custom panels, interactive games, reward systems and the NEXUS Redemption Control Center. That experience led to broader tools with their own interfaces, release workflows and owner-controlled publishing.

   

    
PROXIMUS ARCHIVESGame-focused workspaces expanded into a larger collection of creative, media and entertainment tools.

    
STRATUM & STARFORGEExperiments in Minecraft tooling, game modification workflows and No Man’s Sky asset and save utilities.

    
RELIC DOMINIONAn original offline arcade universe that grew to include customizable relics, rewards and creator tools.

    
SONIC REACTOR IIMusic visualization and audio-reactive artwork that pushed the design of the wider software ecosystem.

    
CHARACTER FOUNDRYProcedural fantasy character artwork, compositing, custom media and creative exports.

    
ASSET ALCHEMISTA browser-based workshop connecting image, animation, audio, game assets, productivity and entertainment across devices.

   

  

  
THE DEVELOPMENT PHILOSOPHY
Build it. Improve it. Make it useful.

   
The focus has never been just collecting feature names. It's about creating tools people can actually use: clear interfaces, convenient exports, personal media controls, repeatable workflows and upgrades that build on what came before.

   
Every new project creates a chance to learn, refine earlier ideas and bring useful features together without leaving people behind on an older version.

  

  
THE VISION
Independent software with room to grow.

   
The long-term goal is a connected family of creative and entertainment applications that feel powerful without being intimidating—accessible on a Windows PC, in a browser, and on a phone wherever that makes sense.

   
The direction remains consistent: keep improving quality, make owner publishing simpler, build features that matter, and turn ambitious concepts into practical tools one release at a time.
