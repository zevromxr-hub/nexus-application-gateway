Rather than treating an application as a collection of features, the goal is to create a cohesive experience: intuitive navigation, useful information, responsive layouts, clear limitations, and updates that preserve what already works.

What NEXUS represents

NEXUS is a collection of independently developed software projects focused on gaming companions, media experiences, creative utilities, and accessible tools. Each project is designed to be approachable without sacrificing depth.

Why Azeroth Companion exists

Azeroth Companion was built to bring talent exploration, official World of Warcraft data research, build planning, and personal organization into one convenient place for players and friends.

Development priorities

Useful features first. Preserve saved progress, keep source-backed game information distinct from estimates, design for PC and mobile, and favor improvements that people can actually use.

Community and continuous improvement

The platform continues to evolve through feature improvements, compatibility fixes, and feedback. New functionality is developed with attention to existing user workflows and data preservation.

Development principles

  • Distinguish verified game information from personal planning and research.
  • Make powerful features approachable through clear and consistent interfaces.
  • Respect users' time, creative freedom, privacy, and saved work.
  • Build long-term value through stable, incremental improvements.
