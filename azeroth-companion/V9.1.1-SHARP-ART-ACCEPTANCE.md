# NEXUS | AZEROTH — SHARP ART v9.1.1 (STAGING)

## What caused the blur
The first five fantasy illustrations are only 380 × 950 pixels each. Their vertical compositions were displayed inside large, wide intro cards and as full cinematic-hero backgrounds. Cropping and enlargement reduced perceived clarity.

## Corrective layout
- Studio destinations are now **portrait cards**, sized below the source artwork's 380px width in the 1380px desktop and 390px mobile regression checks.
- Five cards form a 5-column gallery on large desktop, then 3, 2 or 1 columns as space narrows.
- The desktop cinematic introduction gets one purpose-built **1600 × 900 WebP background**, while the currently selected 380px portrait is shown separately in a max-360px framed panel on the right.
- Phone scenes retain native portrait imagery and show the galleries one at a time without artificial stretching.
- Removed the intentional delay before displaying the preloaded portrait images; retained all existing portal links, saved presets, login features and performance settings.
- Changes are CSS/layout and first-load artwork activation only, with no modification to data or authentication.
