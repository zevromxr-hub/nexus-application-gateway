WHAT THIS VAULT PROTECTS

Real content encryption

Your video bytes, names and notes are encrypted in browser storage before saving. A different visitor opening this page sees their own separate local library—not yours.

Not a cloud video host

Files are not uploaded to GitHub Pages or Cloudflare and will not automatically appear on your other devices. Move libraries with an encrypted backup.

Storage and device limits

Safari, iPhone Home Screen apps and desktop browsers have different storage limits. Private browsing and clearing website data may erase the vault; keep a backup outside the browser.
