Installation basics

For manual Windows installation: download the correct addon release from its official provider, extract the addon folder(s) into your World of Warcraft installation's relevant Interface/AddOns directory, and restart or reload the game. Folder names differ between Retail and Classic installations. Do not run unexpected executables, and follow each provider's instructions.
