# NEXUS | AZEROTH COMPANION v9.1.0 — CITADEL AWAKENING
**Status: staging. Do not announce stable without regression acceptance.**

## New startup behavior
- Fresh canonical application visits route to the lightweight citadel-entry.html page before full application JS/CSS loads.
- Enter Full Companion uses ?enter=1 to bypass the first-visit entrance. ?gateway=1 forces it.
- Functional deep links open the existing app directly, preserving studio targets and account flows.
- Five themed fantasy chambers navigate directly into their respective original working studios.
- Search, up to three pinned destinations and recent chamber/view resume are local browser preferences, not account records.
- Cinematic/Balanced/Performance respects reduced motion and reuses the original v9 visual mode key.
