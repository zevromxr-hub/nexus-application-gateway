Privacy & Data Guide

This is an independent, unofficial World of Warcraft companion. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Blizzard Entertainment.

Manual profiles and local work

Character notes, manual plans, and Talent Forge drafts are designed to be stored in the browser profile on the current device. They are not automatically synchronized to another phone or PC. Browser storage can be erased by site-data removal, private browsing, or device changes. Export a backup before clearing browser data.

Connected services

Features that connect to Battle.net or retrieve Blizzard game data require network requests. The companion depends on a third-party web hosting provider for website delivery and may use an externally hosted authentication/API service for enabled connected features. Those providers can process network and technical request information under their own terms. Connecting your Battle.net account is optional for manual planning features.

Media imports and external content

Locally selected media may be stored in browser storage or handled through browser-managed media features. External images, embedded videos, streaming content, and outbound links may contact third-party services. Do not upload or share content unless you have permission to use it.

Data management

Use the existing Data & Backup section to export, import, or reset the companion data it manages. Talent Forge may have separate save/export and recovery controls. Reset actions are not reversible without a backup. Removing the website from your home screen is not a guaranteed way to remove all associated browser data.

Limits of this guide

This page explains the application's designed data behavior; it is not a verified inventory of every request made by third-party integrations. The project should undergo a fuller privacy and security review before commercial distribution or app-store submission.

Official-game distinction

Talent planning and source-linked paths are research tools. Verify build legality, requirements, and gameplay effects in World of Warcraft. Blizzard and World of Warcraft are trademarks of their respective owner.
