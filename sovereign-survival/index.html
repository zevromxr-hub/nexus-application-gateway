◇ THE WARDEN'S INITIATION

LEARN TO SURVIVE.

01 / MOVE

Never stop moving.

PC: WASD or Arrow keys. iPhone: drag the joystick on the lower left.

02 / ATTACK

Your blades seek prey.

Attacks auto-target nearby enemies. On PC you may also hold the mouse to aim.

03 / EVADE

Dash through danger.

Press Shift on PC or tap DASH on iPhone, even while standing still. Dash follows your current or last movement direction and continues if you release the stick. Watch the cooldown ring.

04 / ASCEND

Harvest soul energy.

Collect glowing orbs to level up, then choose one of three powers. Every fourth wave brings a boss.

05 / RIFT

Leave the warning circle.

Beginning on Wave 3, runic floor rifts warn with a bright expanding ring before releasing damage. Step outside their radius.

06 / TELEGRAPH

Read the enemy's attack.

Hexers mark a line of fire. Ravagers aim before charging. The Void Monarch locks its volley angle before release—dodge across the path.

⚒ ASCENSION ARMORY — Earn Renown on completed runs. Select unlocked champions and equip up to two forged relics before battle. No account needed.

✦ CHAMPION AWAKENING — Press Q on PC or tap the POWER rune on iPhone to cast your champion ability. Every ability has its own cooldown. Reach Rank 3 in Bloodsteel Edge, Arcane Barrage or Runic Ward to evolve your weapons and defense.

♛ SOVEREIGN LEGACY — Open the Legacy hall from the title menu to claim achievements, view the complete champion model and forge purely cosmetic armor and weapon finishes. Elites and bosses occasionally drop Eclipse Shards, which are banked when a run ends in defeat.

✦ CHARACTER ASCENSION — Cast your champion's active ability three times within the same run to awaken its stronger form: Warden Supernova, Sentinel Eternal Winter, or Reaver Dragonfire. Weapon skins are cosmetic; mastery is earned during battle and resets for each new run.

ϟ COMBAT DOMINION — Level up to discover Chain Tempest, Riftstrike and Soul Dominion. Unlock and forge their permanent Power Relics in the Armory to begin future runs with rank 1. Auto mode prioritizes smooth combat; use Eco for lower battery usage.

◈ REALM EXPEDITIONS — Shattered Citadel: defend the Wardstone. Ashen Rift: destroy two portals with your attacks. Moonlit Ruins: stand near the shrine until the ritual charges. Void Throne: stand at both seals to weaken the Void Tyrant, then dodge the dangerous outer-arena pulse. Objectives grant bonus Renown on run completion.
